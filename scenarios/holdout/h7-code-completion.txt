# Dynamic exposed: the extreme prefill-bound corner with the highest service-time variability any
# public trace shows. 111:1 input:output, output CV 3.30, p50 of 8 output tokens against a mean of
# 22.7. Requests are short-lived and arrive at 159 per second, so batch composition turns over
# completely several times a second.
#
# It is also the only mixture in the suite that can reach the 99.9% cap docs/arena.md section 1 names:
# at freeze time round_robin attained 0.9991 at 71 rps and 0.9962 at this file's 159 rps, while every
# chat and long-context mixture tops out near 0.996 at any rate. The reason is arithmetic rather than
# policy — 2,511 tokens is 89 ms of prefill against a 1 s budget, so the prompt-length tail does not
# breach the TTFT SLO the way a 12 k-token chat mixture's does.
#
# Why it is in the suite: it inverts the assumption the chat scenarios share. Here decode is almost
# free and the fleet is a prefill machine, so a policy tuned to decode-side signals — running
# sequences, resident KV — is reading a quantity that barely moves. calibration.md 3.2 calls the
# input:output ratio "the highest-leverage workload parameter in the whole simulator" and warns that
# "any conclusion drawn on ShareGPT (1.1:1) transfers to agentic traffic (100:1) not at all"; this
# file and h6 are the two ends of that axis inside the frozen suite.
