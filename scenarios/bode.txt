# M7 control analysis (U33): the staleness loop as a delayed feedback system. least_requests reads a
# snapshot D = telemetry_delay_ms = 200 ms old, refreshed every I = telemetry_interval_ms = 1000 ms,
# so the loop's effective delay is about D + I/2 = 0.7 s and a pure delay of that length reaches a
# 180 degree phase lag at 1 / (2 * 0.7) = 0.71 Hz. Demo 18 sweeps perturb_frequency_hz and
# bench/bode.py fits the fleet queue's gain and phase against offered load at each point.
# 240 s with 30 s of warm-up gives two full cycles at the lowest frequency, 0.01 Hz.
#
# Demo 2's sweep base. Kept at 32 replicas: at 256 even 100 ms of telemetry staleness is past the
# herding cliff (SLO 0.15 at 100 ms against 0.79 here), so the sweep no longer shows the curve that
# is the finding. Demo 1's least_requests leg is route_least_requests.txt at the 256-replica default.
# Issao, 2026-09-07; the cliff moving with fleet size is finding 10 (U98).
